Choose **Personal** or **Shared**, then open **Transactions**. Use **Reporting month** to choose the month you want to see.

## Find a transaction

![The transactions list with search and two filters above a pending fuel purchase.](/learn/transactions/find-transactions.png "Search and filter the transaction list")

1. In **Search transactions**, enter part of a merchant name, description, or note.
2. Use **All accounts** to show transactions from one account.
3. Use **All categories** to show transactions assigned to one category. A split transaction appears when one of its parts uses that category.
4. A charge marked **Pending** is still waiting to post at the bank. Budget Together shows it separately from finalized totals. When the bank sends a posted replacement, it takes the place of the pending entry.

If you see **No matches on this page. Check the next page.**, select **Next page** to keep looking. Select **First page** to return to the beginning. To clear the filters, empty the search box and choose **All accounts** and **All categories**.

Select a transaction’s description or category to open **Transaction details**. For steps to change its type, category, or splits, see [Review and split transactions](/learn/review-and-split-transactions).

## Add a manual transaction

Select **Add transaction**. You’ll need an active account in this budget. If there are no accounts to choose from, open **Accounts & sharing** to connect a bank.

![The Add a transaction form filled out for a grocery receipt.](/learn/transactions/add-manual-entry.png "Record a grocery receipt")

1. Choose the **Account**.
2. Enter a **Description**.
3. Enter the **Amount**. Use a positive amount for money out. Use a negative amount for money in, including refunds.
4. Set the **Date**. The entry appears in the month you choose here.
5. Choose a **Classification**. For **Purchase or refund**, choose a category under **Category splits**. You can also add a **Note**.
6. Select **Save transaction**.

## Edit or delete a manual transaction

Manual entries show **Manual** under their description.

1. Select the manual entry to open **Transaction details**. To edit it, update the details and select **Save transaction**. The **Account** is fixed once the entry is created.
2. To remove it, select **Delete manual entry**, then confirm with **Delete transaction**.

## Match a manual entry to a bank import

If the bank imports a transaction you already entered, open the manual entry and find **Match an imported entry**. The imported transaction must have the same amount and be in the same account and month.

![The matching section with the Maple Market bank import selected.](/learn/transactions/match-imported-entry.png "Match a receipt to its bank import")

1. Under **Imported transaction**, choose the matching bank entry. Compare the date, description, and amount. Use **Next page of imports** or **First page of imports** if you need to look through more entries.
2. Select **Match entries**. Budget Together keeps the bank entry and applies the manual entry’s type, categories, and note. The manual entry stops counting.
