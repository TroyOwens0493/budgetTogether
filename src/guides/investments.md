A holding is an investment in an account, such as a stock, fund, bond, or CD. In **Projections**, you can import holdings or add them yourself. [Set up your savings forecast](/learn/projections) to see cash savings and investments together.

## Connect an investment account

Open **Projections** and scroll to **Investment accounts**. This is where you manage the investments used in your forecast.

![Investment accounts with numbered highlights for connecting, loading returns, adding or editing a holding, and including an account.](/learn/investments/account-controls.png "Manage investments in Projections")

1. Select **Connect investments** and follow the prompts to link your institution. If asked to check for duplicates, review the matches and select **Confirm accounts**. New linked accounts start in **Personal**.
2. Select **Load historical returns** to look up past returns for stocks and funds with trading symbols.
3. Select **Add holding** to add another investment to a manual account.
4. Select **Edit forecast** next to a holding to change its forecast assumptions.
5. Clear the checkbox next to an account name to leave that whole account out of the forecast.

To move a linked account to your shared budget, open **Accounts & sharing** in **Personal**. Select **Options** next to the account, choose **Shared** in **Destination budget**, and select **Save**. Use **Accounts & sharing** to **Sync** or **Reconnect** a connection.

## Add a manual investment

Select **Add investment manually** in **Projections**. You can create a manual account with its first holding or pick a manual account you already added.

![The Add investment form filled out for a fund in a new manual brokerage account.](/learn/investments/manual-investment.png "Add the first holding in a manual account")

1. Under **Investment account**, leave **New manual account** selected to create an account, or choose an existing manual account.
2. For a new account, enter an **Account name** and choose an **Account type**, such as **Brokerage** or **IRA**.
3. Enter the **Investment name**.
4. Choose the **Investment type**. For a publicly traded stock, ETF, or mutual fund, enter its **Trading symbol** if you want to use historical returns.
5. Enter the **Current investment value** in US dollars. Use what the holding is worth today. Fill in any rate, contribution, or bond or CD terms you want to use in the forecast.
6. Select **Save investment**.

For linked holdings, the institution supplies the name, type, symbol, and value. You can still edit the forecast assumptions.

## Set returns and monthly contributions

For a stock, ETF, or mutual fund, the historical return turns past performance into a single yearly rate. **Load historical returns** uses about 10 years of history, or the available history for newer holdings. It needs at least one year of prices. You can enter your own estimate if the lookup fails or the holding has no trading symbol.

Select **Edit forecast** next to the holding you want to change.

![The Edit investment forecast form with a 6.5 percent annual return override and a $400 monthly contribution.](/learn/investments/edit-forecast.png "Edit a holding's forecast assumptions")

1. Enter **Annual return override (%)** to use your own yearly rate. Clear this field and save to use the historical rate again.
2. Enter the amount to add each month in **Monthly investment contribution**. The sum for all included holdings must fit within this month's saved savings target. The rest of the target goes to cash savings.
3. Clear **Include this holding in the forecast** to leave out this one holding and its contribution.
4. Select **Save investment**.

## Enter bond terms

For a manual bond, choose **Bond** in **Investment type**. For a linked bond, use **Edit forecast** to check or fill in its terms. Use the dates and rates from your bond statement.

![A bond form with numbered highlights on the face value, coupon rate, payment frequency, next payment, and maturity.](/learn/investments/bond-terms.png "Enter a bond's payment terms")

1. Enter the **Total bond face value**, the amount due back when the bond matures. **Current investment value** is what the bond is worth today.
2. Enter the **Annual coupon rate (%)**, the interest rate on the face value. For a zero-coupon bond, enter 0 and skip the payment schedule.
3. In **Coupon payments per year**, choose how often you receive interest. For example, **2 (semiannual)** means twice a year.
4. Enter the **Next coupon payment** date. It must be after today and on or before maturity.
5. Enter the **Maturity date** and select **Save investment**.

The forecast holds the bond's current value steady until maturity. It adds each interest payment to cash when due, then adds the face value to cash at maturity.

## Enter CD terms

A CD is a certificate of deposit. For a manual CD, choose **CD** in **Investment type**. For a linked CD, use **Edit forecast** to check or fill in its terms.

![The CD form with numbered highlights on its annual yield and maturity date.](/learn/investments/cd-terms.png "Enter a CD's yield and maturity")

1. Enter the **CD annual yield (APY %)** from your statement. APY is the yearly rate that includes the effect of earning interest on interest.
2. Enter the **Maturity date** and select **Save investment**. The forecast compounds the interest until that date, then moves the full value to cash.

Add a separate holding for each new bond or CD purchase. Monthly investments apply to holdings without fixed terms.

## Fix missing or mismatched data

- If an account's reported holdings differ from its balance, select **Sync** for the connection in **Accounts & sharing**. If the mismatch continues, clear the account's checkbox under **Investment accounts** until the data is complete.
- If a holding has no current value, sync the account. If a rate or term is missing, use **Edit forecast** to enter it. To leave out a holding that still has a problem, clear **Include this holding in the forecast** and select **Save investment**.
- Unsupported holdings, such as crypto and derivatives, can appear in the account list. The forecast leaves these out.
- If lowering the current month's savings target leaves too much assigned to investments, reduce the **Monthly investment contribution** amounts or update and save the current month's plan.
