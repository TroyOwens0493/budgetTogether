Open **Projections** in the budget you want to forecast. The **Cash savings** and **Investments** charts start with the balances you include and estimate how they could change. The forecast shows amounts before taxes and assumes the same rates and monthly contributions throughout.

Withdrawals, inflation, employer matches, contribution limits, and events like early CD withdrawals or bond defaults are outside the forecast.

## Set your ages and cash assumptions

Select **Forecast settings** at the top of **Projections**. If you see **Set up forecast**, you can select that button too.

![Forecast settings with five numbered highlights for ages, extra cash, interest, and saving.](/learn/projections/forecast-settings.png "Forecast settings")

1. Enter your **Current age** in whole years. The forecast starts today at the age you enter. Update it when needed.
2. Under **Forecast to age**, enter the age where you want the forecast to end.
3. Enter **Additional unlinked cash savings** for cash you want to forecast outside the linked accounts you include. Count each balance once.
4. Enter **Cash annual interest (%)**. This is the rate the forecast applies to your cash savings. Enter 0 for no interest.
5. Select **Save forecast settings**.

## Check how much you plan to save

**Projections** repeats the saved savings target from the current calendar month in your budget's reporting time zone. Changing **Reporting month** elsewhere leaves this forecast on the current month.

![A monthly savings target of $850 split into $500 for investments and $350 for cash savings.](/learn/projections/monthly-target.png "Where your monthly savings target goes")

1. **MONTHLY SAVINGS TARGET** shows the amount the forecast uses each month. The month on the label shows which plan supplies the target.
2. **To investments** shows how much you assigned to the holdings you include.
3. **To cash savings** shows what is left from the target. The forecast adds contributions at the end of each forecast month.
4. Select **Edit monthly budget** to open this month's plan. Save a balanced plan to use its target in the forecast.

## Choose your cash accounts

Under **Cash savings accounts**, check each account whose balance you want to include. Clear the box to leave that balance out. Savings accounts start checked. Checking accounts start unchecked.

![The cash accounts panel with a checked savings account, an unchecked checking account, and the starting cash total.](/learn/projections/cash-accounts.png "Choose which cash balances to include")

1. The checked savings account is selected. Its balance counts when the bank supplies a usable value.
2. **Checking** is unchecked in this example.
3. **Starting cash** combines usable balances from the checked accounts and any additional unlinked cash you entered.

## Read the forecast

**Total at age** shows the combined value of cash savings and investments at the end of the forecast. **New contributions** shows how much the forecast adds from your savings target. **Estimated growth** shows how much interest and investment gains or losses change your total.

![The cash savings and investment charts with both sliders set to age 36.5.](/learn/projections/month-sliders.png "Look at a month on both charts")

1. Move **Explore cash savings by month** to see the cash value for an earlier month. The far left shows your starting balance.
2. Move **Explore investments by month** to see the value of your investments at a different point. Set both sliders to the same age to compare the two balances.
3. The selected age appears next to the chart heading. The dollar amount below the heading shows the estimate for that month.

In this example, a CD matures at age 36. Its value moves from **Investments** to **Cash savings**.

![The yearly table with rows from age 34 to 39 and columns for cash savings, investments, and total.](/learn/projections/yearly-values.png "Compare balances by age")

1. Open **Yearly forecast values** to compare both balances and their total at each whole age.
2. Read the last row for the balances at your forecast end age.

## Resolve forecast warnings

If you see **Complete your forecast**, follow the messages to get your charts back.

![The forecast banner with a missing cash balance, mismatched holdings, and a missing investment return.](/learn/projections/forecast-warnings.png "Examples of problems that pause the forecast")

1. **Complete your forecast** lists what needs attention. If your settings are missing, select **Set up forecast**. If your current plan is unsaved or out of balance, use **Edit monthly budget** to fix and save it.
2. If a bank reports no usable cash balance, open **Accounts & sharing** and select **Sync** for that connection. The forecast needs a balance of zero or more in US dollars. You can also clear the account's box under **Cash savings accounts**.
3. If the value of the reported holdings differs from the investment account balance, sync the connection or clear the account's box under **Investment accounts**.
4. If a holding is missing a return or bond or CD terms, open **Edit forecast** for that holding and fill in the missing information.

If contributions exceed your savings target, lower the contributions or update and save the current month's plan. See [Add investments to your forecast](/learn/investments) for steps to edit a holding.
