A monthly plan gives each dollar a place for one calendar month. It includes your expected income, a category limit for each type of spending, and a savings target.

## Enter your monthly amounts

1. If you have two budgets, choose **Personal** or **Shared**. Open **Monthly budget**.
2. Use **Reporting month** at the top of the page to choose the month you want to plan.
3. Fill in the amounts under **Monthly spending**.

![The Monthly spending form with highlights for income, a category limit, Funds, and the savings target.](/learn/monthly-budget/monthly-spending.png "A monthly plan for October")

1. In **Expected take-home income**, enter what you expect to receive after taxes and deductions.
2. Enter a category limit for each spending category, such as **Groceries**. Enter zero for a category you do not plan to use.
3. If you have set up any funds for the month, the **Funds** category shows the total they need. To change that amount, update the goals in [Funds](/learn/funds).
4. In **Monthly savings target**, enter how much you plan to save for the month apart from your funds. This is a planning amount.

If you have a shared budget, you enter household income there, and your private budget gets a personal allocation from it. See [Shared budgets](/learn/shared-budgets).

## Balance and save your plan

![The plan summary highlights available money, a zero balance, and the Save budget button.](/learn/monthly-budget/balanced-plan.png "A balanced plan ready to save")

1. **Money available to plan** adds **Expected income**, **Incoming budget funding**, and any **Carry-forward**.
2. **Left to allocate** subtracts **Outgoing budget funding**, **Spending limits**, **Funds**, and your **Savings target**. If the amount is positive, assign more to a category or to savings. If it is negative, lower one or more of those amounts.
3. When **Left to allocate** is $0.00, select **Save budget**. **Budget saved.** confirms the save and the budget reserves this month's amounts for any funds.

## Plan the next month

When you open a month for the first time, Budget Together uses the latest earlier plan to fill in the starting amounts. Review the income, limits, and savings target. The **Funds** amount is calculated for the month you open. Balance the plan and select **Save budget** to reserve that month's fund amounts.

To set up the next month from the one you are viewing, select **Copy limits to** followed by the next month's date, below the plan. Then choose that month in **Reporting month** to finish and save. If you see **Next month already has a budget. Open it to make changes there.**, use **Reporting month** to open it and make your changes there.

See [Monthly review and carry-forward](/learn/carry-forward) to check last month's results.

## Fix a message before saving

![The plan summary shows Check amounts and a message to fix the entries before saving.](/learn/monthly-budget/check-amounts.png "An amount needs attention")

1. **Check amounts** appears where the summary cannot calculate a value from your entries.
2. If you see **Check the amounts before saving your budget.**, look for the message under the affected field. Enter zero or a positive amount with at most two decimal places.

- **Adjust your plan until this amount is zero.** Check **Left to allocate**, then adjust your spending limits or savings target.
- **This plan changed. Your unsaved entries are still here.** Note the amounts you want to keep. Select **Load current plan**, then reenter your changes.
- **Fund amounts changed. Check the Funds row, then save the budget again.** Check the updated **Funds** amount, bring **Left to allocate** back to zero, and save again.
