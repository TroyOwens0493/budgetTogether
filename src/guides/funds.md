Use a fund to set money aside for a repeating payment, such as car insurance, or a one-time savings goal, such as a vacation. Each fund belongs to the budget you have open.

## Add a fund

Choose the starting month in **Reporting month**, then open **Funds** and select **Add fund**. Enter a **Fund name** you will recognize in **Transactions**.

![The Add fund form shows the settings for a car insurance payment due every six months.](/learn/funds/add-fund.png "A fund for a repeating payment")

1. Choose a **Fund type**. Choose **Repeating payment** for a bill that comes due every few months or years.
2. Enter the **Payment amount**.
3. Choose the **Next payment month**.
4. Enter the interval in **Pay every (months)**. For example, enter 6 for a bill you pay twice a year.
5. In **Amount already saved**, enter any money you already set aside, or leave it at zero. This is the fund's opening amount. It counts from the starting month and uses none of that month's income.
6. Select **Save fund**.

For a one-time goal, enter the **Goal amount** and choose one of these options under **Fund type**.

- **Savings goal with a target month** calculates how much you need each month to reach the goal, including the target month.
- **Savings goal with a monthly amount** lets you enter a **Monthly amount** to put toward the goal. The final amount can be smaller if you are close to the goal.

## Reserve money in your monthly budget

![The Funds summary highlights the amount required this month and the amount already reserved in this budget.](/learn/funds/reserve-funds.png "Fund amounts before saving the monthly budget")

1. **Required this month** is the total your funds need for the selected month. The automatic **Funds** category in **Monthly budget** uses the same amount.
2. **Reserved in this budget** shows how much the saved plan for that month has already set aside. To reserve the current amounts, open **Monthly budget**, balance the plan, and select **Save budget**. Saving again replaces that month's reservations.

See [Monthly budget](/learn/monthly-budget) for help balancing your plan.

## Read your Fund balance

![The Car insurance card highlights the monthly amount needed, the reserved balance, the current month's reservation, and Edit.](/learn/funds/fund-balance.png "Car insurance after saving the October budget")

1. **required per month** shows what the fund needs for the selected month. For a dated goal, the amount can rise if you skip a month.
2. **Reserved balance** is your fund balance through the selected month. It starts with your opening amount and adds amounts from saved plans. Linked posted purchases subtract from it and linked posted refunds add back to it. Budget Together tracks these amounts without moving money between bank accounts.
3. **Reserved in 2026-10** shows the amount set aside in the October plan alone. Here it adds $100 to the $200 already saved.
4. Select **Edit** to change the fund's settings.

## Link a purchase or refund

Open **Transactions** and select the purchase or refund. Set **Classification** to **Purchase or refund** and choose a category.

![Transaction details shows Car insurance selected for a category split and the Save transaction button.](/learn/funds/link-a-payment.png "Linking a payment to Car insurance")

1. Under **Category splits**, open the menu that says **Use monthly category budget** and choose the fund. If you split the transaction, you can choose a fund for each part it covers.
2. Select **Save transaction**. Once posted, the linked amount still appears in spending reports and uses the fund balance instead of the regular category limit.

To link a refund, use the same steps and choose the same fund. If you enter a refund manually, use a negative amount.

## Edit a fund

1. Use **Reporting month** to choose when the change should start.
2. Open **Funds**, select **Edit** on the fund, and change the name, amount, or schedule.
3. Select **Save fund**. Check **Funds** in **Monthly budget**, then balance and save that budget again if the amount changed.

The opening amount stays fixed. A repeating payment stays a repeating payment. Fund deletion is currently unavailable in the app.
