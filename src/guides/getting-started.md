Budget Together helps you plan your spending and savings. You get a private budget that only you can access. You can also share one budget with one partner. Budget Together never moves money.

## Create your account

On the sign-in screen, select **Create an account**. Then complete the form.

![The Create your account form with five numbered highlights.](/learn/getting-started/create-account.png "The registration form")

1. Enter your **Name**.
2. Enter your **Email**.
3. Choose a **Password** with at least 12 characters.
4. Select **Create account**.
5. You can also select **Learn** from this screen or the sign-in screen.

## Set up your private budget

After you create your account, Budget Together asks you to set up your private budget.

1. Check **Your name**.
2. Check the **Reporting time zone**. Budget Together suggests the time zone from your device.
3. Select **Create my private budget**.

To import transactions, open **Accounts & sharing** and select **Connect a bank**. New bank accounts start in your private budget. To plan your first month, open **Monthly budget**.

> [!TIP]
> To invite a partner, open **Accounts & sharing**. Under **Start a shared budget**, enter your **Partner’s email** and select **Send invitation**. Your shared budget starts when your partner accepts.

## Find your way around

![The desktop sidebar with the budget switcher and nine numbered highlights.](/learn/getting-started/sidebar.png "The desktop sidebar when you have a shared budget")

1. When you have a shared budget, select **Personal** for your private budget or **Shared** for the budget you share with your partner.
2. **Overview** shows money in, money out, savings, and category balances.
3. **Transactions** lets you review, categorize, and add transactions.
4. **Monthly budget** is where you set expected income, category limits, and your savings target.
5. **Funds** helps you set money aside for repeating payments and one-time goals.
6. **Projections** estimates how your cash savings and investments could grow.
7. **Accounts & sharing** is where you connect a bank, manage accounts, or invite a partner.
8. **Profile** is where you change your name, time zone, or password.
9. **Learn** opens these guides.

## Choose a month and check alerts

![The top of the mobile Overview screen with three numbered highlights.](/learn/getting-started/mobile-toolbar.png "The toolbar on a phone")

1. On a phone, tap **Open navigation**, the three-line button, to see the menu.
2. Select the **Alerts** bell to see **Your alerts** for the selected budget and month. A red dot means you have unread alerts.
3. Use **Reporting month** to choose the month you want to see. On a computer, the bell and month picker are at the top right.

> [!NOTE]
> **Projections** uses the current calendar month. The **Reporting month** picker is hidden on that screen.

## Open Learn on your phone

![The open mobile menu with highlights for Learn and Close navigation.](/learn/getting-started/mobile-menu.png "The menu on a phone")

1. Tap **Learn** at the bottom of the menu.
2. Tap **Close navigation**, the X button, to return to the current budget screen.
