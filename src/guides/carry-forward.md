## Check last month's results

1. Open **Overview** and use **Reporting month** to choose the month you want to review.
2. Check **Money in**, **Money out**, and **Saved this month**. Compare **Category balances** with the limits in your Monthly plan.
3. Open **Transactions** to fix any incorrect classifications. Return to **Overview** to check the updated results.

![The October Overview shows money in, money out, and savings above the target.](/learn/carry-forward/month-results.png "October's results before any carry-forward")

1. **Money out** shows posted spending. The line below it shows pending amounts separately.
2. **Saved this month** shows Actual savings. In this example, the household saved $910.
3. The amount above your target accounts for fund reservations and payments. Here, $240 was set aside for funds and a $200 payment came from a fund. That leaves $870 before the $500 Savings target, or $370 above it.

## Understand Available leftovers

Available leftovers start with the part of your savings above the Savings target, after accounting for fund reservations and payments covered by funds. Any amount already assigned as Carry-forward reduces what's available to assign. A month with no savings above the target has no new leftovers to assign.

Carry-forward assigns some of those leftovers to the following month's Category limits or Savings target. Those dollars become Reserved funds for the next month. Retained savings are the part you keep from the source month after the reservations. The original **Saved this month** amount stays the same when money is reserved for a later month.

## Check existing Carry-forward

Budget Together currently lets you see existing Carry-forward. Assigning or changing it is unavailable in the app.

To check what reached the next month, open **Monthly budget** and choose that month in **Reporting month**.

![The November plan summary shows $250 on the Carry-forward row.](/learn/carry-forward/next-month-funding.png "Carry-forward in the next month's plan")

1. **Carry-forward** shows the amount reserved from the previous month. In this example, $150 went to **Groceries** and $100 went to the **Monthly savings target**. The next month's plan includes both amounts in those fields and adds $250 to **Money available to plan**.

## Check alerts and notices

An unread over-budget alert also appears on **Monthly budget** for the selected month. Select the **Alerts** bell to see category alerts, including categories that have used at least 80% of their limit.

![A Groceries over-budget notice with the dismiss button highlighted.](/learn/carry-forward/over-budget-notice.png "An unread notice on Monthly budget")

1. Select the X to dismiss the notice. Check the underlying spending in **Transactions**.

If a correction to last month's transactions, Savings target, or fund amounts leaves too little to cover a reservation, the source month shows a warning under **Your alerts**. You may also see **Carry-forward from 2026-10 is invalid. Check that month’s transactions and savings target.** when you save the next month's budget. The date in the message identifies the month to review.

![Your alerts shows a warning that the source month's savings no longer cover carry-forward.](/learn/carry-forward/carry-forward-alert.png "An alert after the source month's totals changed")

1. Check the source month's posted transactions, Savings target, and fund amounts. Correct any entries that are wrong.
2. **Mark read** clears the unread state for category alerts. The Carry-forward warning stays active while the reservation exceeds the available savings.
