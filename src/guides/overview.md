Choose **Personal** or **Shared**, then select **Overview**. Use **Reporting month** at the top to choose the month you want to check.

## Check the month’s totals

![Three cards showing money in, money out, and savings for one month.](/learn/overview/month-summary.png "The monthly summary")

1. **Money in** shows actual income plus any incoming budget funding for the month. The smaller line shows your expected income and planned budget funding.
2. **Money out** shows counted spending after refunds. Pending purchases appear separately below the total.
3. **Saved this month** shows actual savings after spending and any outgoing budget funding. The smaller line compares savings with your target after accounting for any fund reservations and payments.

To change your expected income, category limits, or savings target, select **Edit monthly plan**.

## Check category balances

![The Category balances card showing a grocery limit, overspending on dining out, and the Manage categories button.](/learn/overview/category-balances.png "Category balances for the selected month")

1. Read the amount spent and the category limit. In this example, you’ve spent $515.56 of your $650.00 grocery limit.
2. Check the amount on the right. **left to spend** means you have money available in the category. A negative amount marked **over budget** means you’ve gone over the limit.
3. Select **Manage categories** to add, rename, or delete a category.

Pending charges stay out of category balances. Purchases paid from a fund use its reserved balance. The **Funds** row shows how much you’ve reserved this month and how much is required.

The **Alerts** bell shows categories that have used at least 80% of their limit or gone over it. Open the bell to see **Your alerts**, then select **Mark read** on an alert you’ve checked.

## Add, rename, or merge categories

Open **Manage categories** in the **Category balances** card.

![The Manage categories dialog with Dining out selected and Other chosen as its replacement.](/learn/overview/manage-categories.png "Editing or replacing a category")

1. In **Category**, choose **Create a category** to add one, or choose an existing category to edit it.
2. Enter the **Name** and choose a **Color**. Select **Create category** for a new one or **Save changes** for an existing one.
3. To delete the selected category, choose a **Replacement**. Its transactions, category limits, and carry-forward allocations move to that category. If both categories had a limit for the same month, the amounts are added together.
4. Select **Delete and reassign**. Check the category names in **Delete category**, then select **Delete and reassign** again.

## Compare spending over time

![A six-month spending chart with colored bars, a spending filter, and a table of monthly amounts.](/learn/overview/spending-chart.png "Spending over time")

1. Use **Spending shown** to switch between **Total spending** and an individual category.
2. Compare the bars for the selected month and the five months before it. On a computer, hover over a colored part of a bar to see the category and its amount.
3. Read the **Spending** column under the chart for exact amounts.

## Open your recent transactions

![The three most recent transactions for the month, including a pending fuel purchase.](/learn/overview/recent-activity.png "Recent activity")

1. **Recent activity** shows up to three transactions from the selected month. Check the date, amount, and whether a transaction is marked **Pending**.
2. Select **All transactions** to open the full list. See [Find and record transactions](/learn/transactions) for search and filter steps.

## Check recurring-charge suggestions

Budget Together looks for at least three similar monthly charges. When it finds a pattern, **Recurring-charge suggestions** appears below **Recent activity**.

![Two recurring-charge suggestions with estimated dates and Confirm and Dismiss buttons.](/learn/overview/recurring-charges.png "Recurring-charge suggestions")

1. Check the merchant, estimated amount, and estimated date.
2. Select **Confirm** if you expect the charge to repeat. Its status changes to **confirmed**.
3. Select **Dismiss** to remove a suggestion from the list.

You still pay the bill as usual.
