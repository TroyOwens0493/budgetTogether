A partner is the other person in your shared budget. You both have equal control of the shared budget. Each of you also has a private budget.

## Invite your partner

Open **Accounts & sharing** in your private budget. Find **Start a shared budget**.

![The invitation form with a sample email and the Send invitation button highlighted.](/learn/shared-budgets/send-invitation.png "Inviting a partner")

1. Enter **Partner’s email**. Use the address they will use to sign in or create an account.
2. Select **Send invitation**.

You can have one pending invitation at a time. The invitation expires after seven days. The card shows the expiration and whether the email is queued, sent, or failed.

![A pending invitation with its expiration, Resend invitation, and Cancel invitation highlighted.](/learn/shared-budgets/pending-invitation.png "Managing a pending invitation")

1. Check **Expires** to see when the link stops working.
2. Select **Resend invitation** if the link expired or sending failed and the address is correct. This replaces the old link and gives the new link seven days.
3. Select **Cancel invitation** to make the link unusable. To fix a wrong address or invite someone else, cancel first, then send a new invitation.

## Review and accept an invitation

Open the invitation email and select **Review invitation**. If prompted, select **Sign in** or **Create an account**. Use the same email address that received the invitation.

![The invitation review showing the signed-in email and the Accept invitation button.](/learn/shared-budgets/review-invitation.png "What your partner sees before accepting")

1. Check the signed-in email under **Shared budget invitation**. If you used a different email, select **Use a different account** and sign in with the invited address.
2. Select **Accept invitation** to create the shared budget. Accepting also cancels any pending invitation you sent to someone else.

You can belong to one shared budget at a time. The new **Shared** budget starts with default categories. It has no saved monthly plans, transactions, or assigned accounts. You can choose which [accounts to move](/learn/bank-accounts).

## Switch between budgets

Use the switcher in the menu after the shared budget is created.

![The budget switcher, with Personal and Shared each highlighted.](/learn/shared-budgets/budget-switcher.png "Choosing a budget")

1. Select **Personal** to see your private budget.
2. Select **Shared** to see the budget you and your partner manage together.

## Set personal funding

From the month you join, plan your income together in **Shared**. Income recorded in your private accounts also counts toward shared income totals. Only you can see your private categories, spending, and individual transactions.

1. Select **Shared**, choose the **Reporting month**, and open **Monthly budget**.
2. Enter the household’s planned income under **Expected take-home income**. Scroll to **Personal budget funding** to set the amounts each person can plan in their private budget.

![Personal budget funding with two personal amount fields highlighted.](/learn/shared-budgets/personal-funding.png "Assigning a personal amount to each partner")

1. Enter the first person’s amount. The example shows **Alice's personal amount**.
2. Enter the second person’s amount. The example shows **Bob's personal amount**. Either partner can see and edit both amounts.

Adjust the shared plan until **Left to allocate** is zero, then select **Save budget**. Each personal allocation reduces shared funding and adds the same amount to that person’s private budget. Budget Together makes no bank transfer.

Select **Personal** and open **Monthly budget** for the same month to use your allocation. If the change affects a saved personal plan, balance it and select **Save budget** again.

## Find historical contributions

Select **Personal**, choose the **Reporting month**, and open **Accounts & sharing**. Look for a heading such as **Historical contributions for 2025-10**. If it appears, you can see the planned contribution and any **Confirmed contributions**. These older records track funding from your private budget to the shared budget. Confirmed amounts come from matched transfers. Some historical records are read-only.

## Leave a shared budget

> [!NOTE]
> Leaving permanently deletes the shared budget and all its records **for both people**. You cannot undo this. Accounts return to their owners’ private budgets. Each person keeps their private records and bank connections.

Select **Shared**, open **Accounts & sharing**, and select **Leave shared budget**.

![The Leave shared budget dialog and its permanent deletion warning for both people.](/learn/shared-budgets/leave-shared-budget.png "Confirming what happens when either partner leaves")

1. Select **Cancel** to keep the shared budget.
2. Select **Leave and delete shared budget** only if you want to delete it for both people.
