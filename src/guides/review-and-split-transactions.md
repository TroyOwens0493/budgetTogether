Open **Transactions** in your private or shared budget. Select a transaction’s description or the button in its **Category** column to open **Transaction details**.

## Find entries that need a check

![Two imported transactions marked Needs review and Classification pending.](/learn/review-and-split-transactions/review-status.png "Classification messages in the transaction list")

1. **Needs review** means Budget Together was unsure of the type or category. Open the transaction to check it.
2. **Classification pending** means Budget Together is still determining the type and category. Totals can change when that finishes. You can open the transaction and save your own choice now.

## Check the type and category

![The Transaction details dialog for a supermarket purchase with Groceries selected.](/learn/review-and-split-transactions/check-classification.png "Review an imported purchase")

1. In **Classification**, choose **Purchase or refund**, **Income**, or **Transfer or credit card payment**. Use the transfer option for money moved between accounts and for credit card payments. Transfers stay out of spending and income totals.
2. For **Purchase or refund**, choose the category under **Category splits**. Choose the category the original purchase used when you record a refund. The refund reduces spending in that category.
3. Select **Save transaction**. This clears **Needs review** or **Classification pending**, even if the suggested type and category were already right.

You can also edit the **Description** and **Note**. The bank sets the imported amount, date, and posting status.

## Split a purchase or refund

Open the transaction and choose **Purchase or refund**. Under **Category splits**, divide the amount across categories or assign part of it to a fund.

![Two split rows for a supermarket purchase, with the Transport portion linked to Car maintenance.](/learn/review-and-split-transactions/splits-and-fund.png "Split a $98.25 purchase")

1. Select **Add split** to add another row. Use the **×** beside a row to remove it.
2. Choose a category for each row. Here, the purchase covers groceries and car supplies, so the categories are **Groceries** and **Transport**.
3. Enter the amount for each row. The amounts must add up to the transaction amount. Use negative amounts for refund splits. Here, $65.25 plus $33.00 equals $98.25.
4. If a split is covered by a fund, use the dropdown that shows **Use monthly category budget** and choose that fund. In this example, the $33.00 split uses **Car maintenance**.

Select **Save transaction**. The list shows **Split · 2 categories** for a two-part split.

You can also choose a fund for a purchase with just one split. Posted payments reduce the fund balance, and linked refunds add money back. Those amounts still appear in spending reports. The fund covers them in place of the regular category limit.
