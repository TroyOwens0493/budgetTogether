## Connect a bank

Open **Accounts & settings** in the menu.

![The bank connection button above three account cards, with two numbered highlights.](/learn/bank-accounts/accounts.png "Accounts & settings")

1. Select **Connect a bank**. In the window that opens, find your bank, sign in, and follow the steps to choose which accounts to connect.
2. Under **Accounts in this budget**, select **Options** on an account to change its name or move it.

You can import transactions from checking, savings, and credit card accounts. New accounts start in your private budget, even if you connect while viewing **Shared**. Select **Personal** to see them if you have a shared budget.

If the app flags a possible duplicate, select **Review accounts** under **Your bank connections** or use the review window if it is already open. Choose **Same account — keep the existing connection** or **Different account — import this account** for each match. Select **Confirm accounts** to finish the review and allow imports to start.

## Move or rename an account

Select the budget that has the account, then open **Accounts & settings**. An account marked **Current assignment** belongs to the selected budget. **Historical records** shows where older transactions are available. Only the person who connected an account can move it.

Select **Options** on the account you want to change.

![Account options showing the account name, destination budget, and Save button.](/learn/bank-accounts/account-options.png "Moving a checking account back to the private budget")

1. Edit **Account name** to rename the account.
2. To move it, pick the **Destination budget**. Choose **Shared · shared** for your shared budget or **My budget · private** for your private budget. Choose **Current budget** to keep it where it is.
3. Select **Save**.

> [!NOTE]
> The move applies to new imports dated on or after the move date. Earlier transactions and entries already imported stay in their original budget. Moving an account to **Shared** keeps your earlier private transactions private.

## Check and fix an import

Open **Accounts & settings** and look under **Your bank connections**. This list shows the banks you connected, whichever budget you are viewing.

![Two bank connections, including a paused import with its last successful import time and recovery controls.](/learn/bank-accounts/bank-connections.png "Checking the status of an import")

1. Check the status and **Last successful import**. **Importing** means an import is in progress. **Not yet** means the bank has no successful import so far.
2. Select **Sync** to request an import. If an import fails, follow the message under the status. **Import paused** means your imported history is still available, but it may be out of date.
3. If you see **Needs reconnection** or the message asks you to sign in again, select **Reconnect**. Follow the bank sign-in steps to restore access. The app then requests another import.
4. Select **Disconnect** to stop importing from that bank.

## Disconnect a bank

Select **Disconnect** next to the bank under **Your bank connections**. Read the confirmation before you continue.

![The Disconnect bank confirmation with the warning that imported history will be kept.](/learn/bank-accounts/disconnect.png "Disconnecting a bank")

1. Select **Disconnect bank** to stop future imports and keep the transactions already imported. Select **Cancel** to keep the bank connected.

Use **Connect a bank** if you want to connect a disconnected bank again.
